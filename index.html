Scout's rules

  • Search any player in the league, past or present. Only accurate ones count as answers.
  • A wrong guess never costs points. Try again until time runs out.
  • This-season prompts use per-game averages through Nov 12, 2026 (Basketball-Reference), minimum 7 games. History prompts use closed lists, such as every MVP, Finals MVP, DPOY, ROY, Sixth Man, Most Improved, No. 1 pick, contest winner and Olympic roster, through 2025.
  • No peeking: after each answer you only see your tier. The full crowd board unlocks when the round ends.
  • The crowd is simulated from how widely known each player is, so stars are crowded and role players are gold.
